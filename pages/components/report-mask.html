What is it about?

This is the only place where your structure leaves your browser. Every conversion — InChI, InChIKey, RInChI, AuxInfo — runs locally and is never uploaded.

Your structure and description, along with the generated InChI, InChIKey, log, and selected options are sent to the Beilstein-Institut for the InChI Trust.
